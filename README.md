## Executive Summary
 
Indigo Airlines operates in a highly competitive aviation market, where booking behavior, passenger preferences, and operational efficiency directly influence revenue and customer experience. The flight booking dataset provided an opportunity to study how route patterns, seat class, travel origin, and add-on services affect booking amount and passenger demand.

Analyze the booking data to identify customer preferences, revenue drivers, route profitability, and operational patterns, and translate those findings into clear business insights that could support scheduling, pricing, and service decisions.

Cleaned and structured the dataset in Excel, defined key metrics and a metric tree, and used pivot tables, slicers, charts, descriptive analysis, and hypothesis testing to explore booking trends, seat class behavior, ancillary service usage, revenue distribution, and flight duration variance. The analysis also covered booking origin, sales channel, trip type, passenger load, and the relationship between planned and actual flight duration.

The analysis showed that business class and ancillary services such as extra baggage contributed strongly to booking value, internet bookings dominated demand, and certain routes generated significantly higher revenue than others. Weekday and morning flights showed stronger passenger load patterns, while variations in actual versus planned flight duration highlighted operational inefficiencies on specific routes. These findings can help Indigo improve route selection, pricing strategy, service design, and overall operational planning.
